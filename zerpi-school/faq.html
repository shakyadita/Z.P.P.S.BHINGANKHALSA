Admissions & Enrollment

Enrollment is done at the school office. Parents or guardians should visit the school during working hours (10:00 AM – 5:00 PM) with the required documents. Our staff will guide you through the admission process step by step.
Early Years (Balwadi/Pre-Primary): Children aged 3 to 5 years are welcome.
Primary (Standard 1): Children must be at least 6 years old by June 15th of the admission year.
Age certificates or birth certificates are required at the time of admission.
As a Zilla Parishad school, education at Z.P.P.S. Bhingankhalsa is provided free of cost. There are no tuition fees. The government also provides free textbooks, mid-day meals and uniforms under various schemes.
Please bring the following documents:
• Birth certificate of the child
• Aadhar card of child and parent/guardian
• Caste certificate (if applicable)
• Previous school leaving certificate (for transfers)
• 2 passport-size photographs
Our school office will advise if any additional documents are needed.
The primary medium of instruction at Z.P.P.S. Bhingankhalsa is Marathi. English is taught as a subject from Standard 1 onwards, and children are introduced to basic English reading and writing progressively through the primary years.

Academic & Curriculum

Z.P.P.S. Bhingankhalsa offers:
• Early Years / Balwadi: Pre-primary education
• Primary: Standard 1 to Standard 4
The school follows the Maharashtra State Board curriculum as prescribed by the Zilla Parishad.
School is open from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday to Saturday. The school is closed on Sundays and public holidays as per the Maharashtra government school calendar.

Campus & Safety

Our campus includes bright and welcoming classrooms, a dedicated playground for sports and physical activity, a school garden where children grow vegetables, a library corner, clean drinking water facilities, and separate washrooms for boys and girls. We are continuously improving our facilities for our students.
Hygiene is a priority at our school. Classrooms and washrooms are cleaned daily. Students are taught personal hygiene — handwashing, dental hygiene and cleanliness — as part of their daily routine. Regular health check-ups are conducted, and we work with our community to maintain a clean and healthy school environment.
The safety of every child is our highest priority. Our school has a secure campus, and children are only released to authorised parents or guardians. Teachers are trained in child safety protocols. We have a strict no-stranger policy, and all visitors must be verified before entering the school premises.
Children should come to school in their uniform, clean and ready to learn. Please pack:
• A water bottle with fresh drinking water
• Stationery (pencil, eraser, ruler)
• School bag with books for that day
• Any materials requested by the teacher for specific activities
Heavy bags are discouraged — we encourage children to bring only what is needed for the day.

Community & Events

Yes! Our Annual Gathering (स्नेहसंमेलन) is a highlight of the school year. Students perform dance, singing, Lezim, drama, and sports events. We also celebrate Republic Day, Independence Day, Shivaji Maharaj Jayanti, Maharashtra Din, and other important national and cultural occasions with enthusiasm and community pride.
We warmly welcome parent involvement! Parents can participate in School Management Committee (SMC) meetings, volunteer for events, and contribute to plantation drives and cleanliness campaigns. Regular parent-teacher meetings keep families connected with their child's progress and school life.
Our students thrive beyond the classroom! We offer sports (Kabaddi, Kho-Kho, athletics), singing and music (morning assembly, patriotic songs), folk dance and Lezim, art and craft-making, eco-gardening, self-directed reading and digital learning, and compassionate community values. These are not extra — they are the heart of our school.
Absolutely. Z.P.P.S. Bhingankhalsa is deeply rooted in the Bhingan Khalsa village community. Our school events — especially the Annual Gathering — bring together the entire village. We conduct tree plantation drives, cleanliness campaigns, and awareness programs that connect our school to our community's wellbeing.
Every child's achievement matters here! We celebrate through merit recognition in morning assembly, performance opportunities at school events, and showcasing student artwork and projects on our school notice board. We believe every child has a unique talent worth celebrating — whether in sports, singing, academics, gardening, or kindness.
