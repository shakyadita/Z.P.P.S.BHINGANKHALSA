DISCOVER OUR SCHOOL

Small school.
Endless possibilities.

Located in the serene village of Bhingan Khalsa, Zilla Parishad Primary School (Z.P.P.S. Bhingankhalsa) provides a safe, vibrant, and loving learning environment where every child is treated with patience and encouragement. We believe that every big dream begins somewhere small.

Rooted in the Community

As an official Zilla Parishad school, we serve the children and families of Bhingan Khalsa, fostering pride in local heritage while offering modern educational opportunities.

Bilingual Foundation (Marathi & English)

Our children learn concepts deeply in their mother tongue Marathi, while building strong early English literacy to equip them for the world beyond.

Curiosity & Digital Readiness

From creative chalkboards and reading corners to interactive smart boards and digital tools, we blend hands-on learning with early technological exposure.
